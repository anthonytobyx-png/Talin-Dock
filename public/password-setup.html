Household cloud sign-in

Choose a unique passphrase and keep it in your password manager. This page creates its verifier entirely in your browser. It sends no requests and stores no password or verifier.

Set the Cloudflare secret

In your Cloudflare Pages project, open Settings → Variables and secrets → Add. Choose Secret, name it TALIN_PASSWORD_HASH, paste this verifier as its value, then save. Redeploy afterward.

Do not send your passphrase or verifier in chat. Close this page after configuring the secret. Anyone with the passphrase can access shared household cloud preferences.
